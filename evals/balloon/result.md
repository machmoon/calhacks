## Flight on a real forecast (Tawhiri, GFS)

`cargo run -p wright-balloon --example sim` asks the public Tawhiri predictor (https://api.v2.sondehub.org/tawhiri) for this design's flight (ascent 5.18 m/s, burst 37819 m, sea-level descent 4.29 m/s) from the launch site at 2026-10-25T16:00:00Z on GFS 2026-10-24T12:00:00Z, recovers the GFS wind profile from Tawhiri's ascent by inverting its own wind model (tawhiri/models.py), and flies Wright's model and a Rust port of Tawhiri's `standard_profile` + RK4 (tawhiri/solver.pyx) on it. Animation: [sim/flight.mp4](sim/flight.mp4), still: [sim/flight.png](sim/flight.png).

| track | landing from launch | flight time |
|---|---|---|
| Tawhiri API (SondeHub), GFS 2026-10-24T12:00:00Z | 65.2 km at 86 deg | 176 min |
| Tawhiri model (Rust port), same GFS winds | 64.2 km at 87 deg | 177 min |
| Wright model, GFS winds 2026-10-24T12:00:00Z | 59.7 km at 87 deg | 177 min |
| Wright model, assumed wind profile | 136.9 km at 90 deg | 177 min |

Wright's landing is 5.7 km from the live Tawhiri prediction and 4.6 km from Tawhiri's model on the same winds (the port lands 1.4 km from the API: the wind recovery is faithful). The difference is the ascent: Tawhiri climbs at a constant rate, Wright's rate comes from free lift and the Gallice Cd(Re) drag of the growing envelope (4.4 m/s at launch, faster aloft), so it spends longer in the low-level winds. Descent agrees within a minute (both use terminal velocity ~ 1/sqrt(rho); Tawhiri's NASA Glenn density vs Wright's US76). Wright keeps its own ascent and descent models and adopts Tawhiri's winds. On these winds every check passes; the eval keeps the assumed profile (it drifts 137 km, twice as far) because a design must also fly on a windier day.

## Why Pat's design fails

* `part_temperature`: phone-backup: box 6..68 C vs rated 0..35 C, ground -10..35 C (cases -10.0 C, 15.0 C, 35.0 C); forecast 15 C included
* `launch_window`: missing evidence: no launch window: the thermal checks fail at 12.5 C, the middle of the -10..35 C range
* `link_secondary`: missing evidence: ELRS telemetry (CR8 + ESP32 + M10): no ground station for this link
* `gps_coexistence`: geprc-vtx-1g2-2w -> matek-m10q at 0.21 m: -16.4 dBm vs -22.0 dBm (fundamental 1280 MHz through patch rejection 30 dB)
* `imaging`: logitech-brio records 3.4 h at the cold but leaves its temperature range; lens 0 mm behind the port, clear cone +-49 deg (need +-30); burst at 1.9 h
* `recovery_probability`: P = 0.828: APRS (DRA818V + ESP32 + M10) 0.90, ELRS telemetry (CR8 + ESP32 + M10) 0.00, phone in the box 0.00

The plan names no balloon, chute, foam wall or fill target, so all 945 combinations in the catalog were tried and the one failing the fewest checks kept (then highest burst, then cheapest); with the earlier assumed choices (HAB-1200, 36 in chute, 1 in wall, 5 m/s) it fails 8.

### Go / no-go (first line of build/checklist.md)

GO / NO-GO: measure the ground air temperature at the launch site. Launch only between -12 C and 35 C (10 F to 95 F); outside that range the payload box leaves a part's rated temperature (the `launch_window` check), so scrub


### Fill procedure (Wright)

1. Tie the Kaymont/Totex HAB-3000 (3000 g) to the filler; hang a weight of **2485 g** (neck lift = gross lift minus the balloon) on the filler.
2. Fill with Helium UHP 5.0, size 300 cylinder (292 ft^3) (99.999 % purity) until the balloon just lifts the filler weight: about 5.24 m^3 (185 ft^3) at 21 C, launch diameter 2.15 m. Buy 1 cylinder(s) of 8.3 m^3.
3. Tie off, attach the chute (Spherachutes 72" Classic) and the payload; the free lift is then 1717 g, giving 4.41 m/s at launch by the US76 + Gallice-drag model (the CUSF rule sized this fill for 5.5 m/s).

### GPS setting

Before flight, set every u-blox receiver to an airborne dynamic platform model (UBX-CFG-NAV5 `dynModel` = 6, airborne <1g, or CFG-NAVSPG-DYNMODEL = AIR1 on M10; u-blox M10 SPG 5.00 interface description UBX-20053845, CFG-NAV5 table) and read it back. In the default portable model u-blox receivers stop reporting above 12 km; the `gps_altitude` check fails a tracker whose `gps_airborne` is false.
