# Before launch

- Proof-test both tanks with water to 66.0 bar (LOX) and 66.0 bar (fuel), 1.5 x MEOP, before any propellant
- Cold-flow the feed system: command the LOX main valve open 1.00 s after igniter light and the fuel main 1.25 s after (LOX lead; propellant reaches the injector later by the 0.30 s valve stroke plus line fill, which the start_sequence check reports)
- Static-fire before flight: confirm Pc 30.0 bar and O/F 1.70 against the engine table; the igniter must burn 4.0 s
- Weigh the vehicle and enter the measured dry mass and CG in the spec (measured_dry); re-run the checks before flying
- Ground-test both altimeters (featherweight_blue_raven, altus_easymini) and the GPS tracker (featherweight_gps_tracker); dual-deploy charges sized and ground-tested for the drogue (fc_classic_elliptical_30in) and main (fc_iris_ultra_compact_72in)
- Rail: the design leaves the rail at 111 ft/s; use the rail length it was checked for and confirm the rail guides slide freely
- Predicted apogee 10077 ft (dispersion band 8857-11318 ft): file it as the contract altitude
