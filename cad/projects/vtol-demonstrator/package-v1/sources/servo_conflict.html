Hitec’s HS-5065MG Digital Micro Servo is engineered for hobbyists who demand exceptional speed and torque performance in a compact footprint. Built for high-performance applications such as electric park flyers, small RC helicopters, and lightweight robotics, this servo offers the reliability and precision needed for tight-space installations. Its digital circuitry delivers faster response and greater holding power than traditional analog servos, giving you enhanced control and efficiency where it matters most.

Featuring durable metal gears and a top-quality amplifier, the HS-5065MG is designed to handle demanding tasks with minimal wear over time. Its rugged design is complemented by a ball bearing-supported output shaft, ensuring smooth and stable operation even under continuous stress. Whether you're upgrading your micro aircraft or refining your compact robotics system, the HS-5065MG delivers exceptional value in a small, high-performance package. Keep it in service with a replacement HS-65MG / HS-5065MG metal gear set, or browse all Hitec micro servos.

NOTE: The HS-5065MG is the digital version of the HS-65MG.

HS-5065MG Key Features

  • Lightweight Design: Weighing in at only 11.9g, the HS-5065MG is optimized for efficient flight and movement.
  • Durable Gear Train: Built with 1 Heavy Duty Resin & 4 Metal Gears, this servo provides smooth and reliable performance under demanding conditions.
  • Cored Metal Brush Motor: Designed with Hitec’s cored motor, the HS-5065MG provides dependable speed, torque, and long service life.
  • Compact Micro Size: With an 11.6mm case size, this servo is the perfect fit for tight installations in small-scale aircraft, helicopters, and robotics applications.
  • Fully Programmable: Via the Hitec DPC-20 and/or HFP-30 digital servo programmers, the performance of the HS-5065MG can be fine-tuned for customized response curves, fail-safe settings, and more.

HS-5065MG Specifications

  • Operating Voltage Range: 3.5V ~ 6.8V (2S LiFe)
  • Speed:
    • 4.8V: 0.14 sec/60°
    • 6.0V: 0.11 sec/60°
  • Torque:
    • 4.8V: 25.0 oz-in (1.8 kgf-cm)
    • 6.0V: 30.6 oz-in (2.2 kgf-cm)
  • Dimensions: 0.93 x 0.46 x 1.18 in (23.6 x 11.6 x 30.0mm)
  • Weight: 0.42 oz (11.9g)
  • Position Sensor Type: Digital
  • Output Spline: 24T

HS-5065MG Ideal Applications

  • Park flyers and indoor 3D aircraft
  • Gliders and small-scale sailplanes
  • Lightweight UAVs and drones
  • Robotics projects requiring micro actuation
  • Educational and STEM kits
